Hiring Model Fairness Audit

A planned audit of a simulated hiring-screening model, focusing on model performance, selection rates, and responsible interpretation.

PythonStatistical analysisFairness metricsI-O Psychology

Research / business question

Does a hypothetical screening model produce different selection outcomes across groups, and how should those differences be assessed?

Project status

Planned. The portfolio does not yet contain computed fairness results.

Proposed approach

Use simulated or properly licensed public data with transparent limitations. Define the selection outcome, compare a simple baseline with a model, and calculate group-level selection rates and relevant error metrics.

Metrics to explain

Consider selection-rate ratios/differences and true-positive/false-positive rates where the data and use case support them. Metrics can conflict; no single metric proves a system is fair.

I-O Psychology context

Discuss job-relatedness, criterion validity, measurement quality, accessibility, adverse-impact concerns, and the need for expert legal and organizational review.

Responsible use

A demo is not a validated hiring tool. Do not use it to screen real candidates. Synthetic data can illustrate calculations but cannot establish real-world fairness.

Deliverables to add

Data and assumptions · Model card · Group metrics table · Visual comparison · Limitations · Recommendations for governance and validation.
