Workforce Retention Intelligence

A planned analysis of whether workforce data can help explain employee turnover patterns and inform responsible retention strategies.

PythonPandasscikit-learnModel explainability

Research / business question

Can historical workforce attributes help identify patterns associated with employee turnover—and what should an organization do with those insights?

Project status

Planned. No model has been trained for this portfolio case study yet.

Proposed approach

Select a public, appropriately licensed HR dataset; document its provenance and limitations; inspect missingness and target balance; establish a baseline; compare a small number of interpretable and tree-based models using held-out data and suitable metrics.

Evaluation plan

Report precision, recall, ROC-AUC where appropriate, confusion matrix, and calibration if relevant. Choose thresholds based on the decision context rather than accuracy alone. Explain the costs of false positives and false negatives.

Organizational interpretation

Distinguish predictive association from causal explanation. Any observed pattern would be a hypothesis for organizational investigation—not proof that a factor causes someone to leave.

Responsible use

Do not use a prototype to rank named employees or make employment decisions. Address privacy, subgroup performance, data representativeness, and the risk of targeting employees instead of improving working conditions.

Deliverables to add

Dataset citation and license · Reproducible notebook · Baseline comparison · Model evaluation plots · Plain-language executive summary · Actual results and limitations.
