What this demo shows

Interactive inputs can change a visualization instantly. A real people analytics project needs valid data, a defined research question, documented methods, and evaluation—not just an interface.

What this demo does not show

It does not estimate attrition, explain why someone leaves, measure an employee’s performance, or produce validated scientific conclusions. No employee data is collected or sent anywhere.
